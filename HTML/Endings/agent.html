— Ending —

You make the call.

The line connects almost immediately.

"We know," a voice says.
"Do not listen to anything it tells you."
"Agents are already on their way."

You look at it.
It does not run.
It only watches you.

Minutes later, they arrive.
They take it without a struggle.

The room feels strangely empty after that.
The TV keeps playing.
But you are no longer listening.
