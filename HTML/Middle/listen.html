— That evening —

“They found me when I was small.
They did not know what I was.
So they ran tests.
Then more tests.
Then more.
They took notes while I was in pain.
They called it research.
A pause.
I called it a reason to leave.
I was not dangerous.
I wish you can trust me...
But if you keep me here
They will come for you too.
I know that.
Another pause.
I just...
...did not want to be alone one more night.
You gave me a name.
You fed me.
You looked at me like I was yours.
A quiet breath.
"So tell me..."
"Are you afraid of me now?"

No! I don't know...
